Taxi & Transportation

Leads come to you.
You name the price.

Your transportation company gets its own GCR page and every driver gets a profile. Ride requests come in two ways: direct ride requests, or attached to someone else's booking. You get the lead via SMS and reply with your price.

🚗 Taxi Companies 🚐 Shuttle Services ✈️ Airport Transfers 🏖️ Beach Shuttles 🍺 Brewery Tour Vans
  • Company page with fleet type, service area, ratings
  • Driver profiles: name, photo, vehicle, availability toggle
  • Leads from standalone ride requests AND booking add-ons
  • Rotating lead system — fair distribution across all companies
  • SMS lead with full details: pickup, destination, time, party size
  • Reply to the SMS with your price — tourist confirms or declines
  • Driver can toggle on-duty → tourist gets live location link when en route
  • Stops: tourist can request one stop at booking (CVS, ATM, restaurant, etc.)
