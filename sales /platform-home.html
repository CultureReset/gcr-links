Built-In AI

Two AIs. One for your
customers. One for you.

Every business on the platform gets two separate AI models — a public-facing assistant that talks to your customers and a private AI that knows your entire business and answers only to you.

Public AI · Customer-Facing

Your 24/7 Front Desk

Powered by open-source Llama/Mistral and trained on your business — hours, prices, services, FAQs, reviews. Talks to customers on your site so you don't have to.

  • Answers questions about pricing, availability, and how to book
  • Trained on all your public pages, services, and reviews
  • Updates automatically when you change your info
  • Runs on your own server — you own the model
  • Smart search: customers can ask "kid-friendly rentals near me" across the whole platform
Private AI · Owner Only

Your Business Brain

Connects to your Google Drive or Dropbox and learns from your private files, notes, and history. Accessible only through your dashboard — Claude-powered, never shared.

  • Ask anything about your business: "What was my busiest month last year?"
  • Reads your private docs, receipts, notes, and booking history
  • You attach your own cloud storage — data stays in your account
  • Billed via Stripe usage metering — pay only when you use it
  • Dashboard AI assistant available now; voice calls coming in v2
