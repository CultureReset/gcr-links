FAQ

The questions businesses are going to ask first.

How is this different from Google reviews?

CyberCheck Reviews ties feedback to a real booking, purchase, or service event. It is designed to create stronger trust than open review systems with no proof.

Can I create my own review questions?

Yes. Businesses can create custom questions instead of relying only on generic star ratings.

Does it work with bookings?

Yes. It is designed for bookings, purchases, checkouts, completed services, and e-commerce orders.

Can I use SMS and email?

Yes. Secure review links can be sent through SMS or email depending on the workflow.

Is this only for restaurants?

No. It can work for restaurants, charters, service businesses, e-commerce, appointments, and more.

Can these reviews appear on my site?

Yes. Verified reviews can be displayed on CyberCheck pages, profile pages, and future partner or discovery pages.
