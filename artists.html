Artist Platform

Give every artist
their own live page.

Musician pages linked directly to the restaurant or bar's QR menu. Fans scan the table, see who's playing tonight, tap the artist's name — and get their full page. Follow. Book. Share.

The Problem

Restaurants promote live music. Artists disappear after the show.

The venue posts "Live music tonight!" — no name, no link, no way for fans to follow the artist. The artist plays, goes home, and gets no digital traction from the gig.

🎸

Artists have no live page

A Facebook page from 2019 and an Instagram with 200 followers. Nowhere to send fans.

📵

Venues can't promote them

"Live music tonight" with no artist link. Fans don't know who's playing or how to follow them.

🔁

No recurring discovery

Every Tuesday they play the same venue. Nobody knows except regulars. No new fan growth.

📅

Booking is chaos

Venues text artists the week before. Artists manage multiple venue schedules in their head. Double-books happen.

How It Works

Artist page. Venue schedule. All connected.

1

Artist gets their page

Name, genre, bio, photo, upcoming shows, social links, booking contact. Live in minutes.

2

Venue adds them to lineup

Manager taps the artist in their weekly lineup update. They're added to the QR menu automatically.

3

Customers scan the QR

"Live Tonight: Jake Morgan" — they tap his name, land on his live page, follow, share, book.

4

Artist grows their following

Every table scan is a potential new fan. The venue promotes the artist every time they're playing.
