Restaurant demo flow

How the restaurant version works

1

Customer books or scans

They come from your website, Gulf Coast Radar, Google, social, a QR code on the table, or a direct link.
2

Source gets tracked

You see whether the lead or reservation came from GCR, Facebook, Google, direct traffic, or a promo link.
3

SMS confirmation and reminder

Customer gets confirmation. Later they get a reminder, update link, or same-day message depending on the setup.
4

Manager updates once

Hours, specials, catch of the day, menu changes, photos, and promos can be updated from one dashboard or simple text prompt.
5

Everything stays synced

Your menu page, QR menu, smart profile, and GCR page all stay aligned instead of drifting out of date.
6

Verified review request goes out

After the visit, the guest gets a one-time review link tied to the actual reservation or visit flow.
