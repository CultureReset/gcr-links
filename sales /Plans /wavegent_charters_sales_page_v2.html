How the charter version works

1

Customer lands on your page

They come from Gulf Coast Radar, Google, Facebook, your links page, an SMS campaign, or a direct booking link.
2

Source gets tracked

You see whether the booking came from GCR, links page, social, direct traffic, or a referral link.
3

Booking gets completed

Guest chooses trip, date, time, and add-ons. Payment runs through your connected setup or custom flow.
4

SMS confirmation goes out

The customer gets confirmation details. The captain gets the trip added to their operating workflow.
5

Morning briefing gets sent

Weather, tide, seas, guest count, and trip time are sent automatically so the operator starts the day prepared.
6

Verified review request goes out

After the trip, the guest gets a one-time review link tied to the actual booking, date, and trip type.
