Why most businesses stay disorganized

The issue is not that owners do not care. The issue is that important updates and follow-ups depend on people remembering to do them manually.

Too many manual steps

Staff has to remember to update the site, update the QR menu, update the specials, send reminders, and keep everything lined up.

Important things slip

Daily specials go stale. Catch of the day never gets updated. Customers do not get follow-up. Bookings happen but the owner still chases messages.
