How to sell the platform

Sell the outcome. Not the architecture.

The easiest way to sell this is to stop describing it like a giant AI ecosystem. Businesses do not buy architecture. They buy more bookings, fewer repetitive questions, better guest prep, better follow-up, and a website that actually helps people choose.

The sales structure

Keep it simple enough that a business owner understands it in under a minute.

1. Open with the pain

“Your website helps people find you, but it stops being helpful right when people still have questions.”

  • Phone calls and repetitive questions
  • Messy pre-arrival support
  • Weak follow-up or low conversions

2. Show the simple fix

“We add a trainable concierge and sales layer to the site that helps people choose faster and gets them into the right booking flow.”

  • Voice or chat interaction
  • Structured data answers
  • Guided next step

3. Close with a pilot

“Let’s test this on a limited group, prove it reduces support load or increases conversion, then expand from there.”

  • No giant rollout first
  • One property / one site / one vertical
  • Measured outcome

The one-line pitch

You should be able to say what it is in one clean sentence.

It’s a trainable sales and concierge agent that uses live business data and authentic customer feedback to help people choose faster, ask fewer repetitive questions, and move into the right booking flow. Use this line first

Who buys this

Different people buy it for different reasons. Tailor the pitch to their pain.

Best early buyers

  • Businesses still handling too much by phone
  • Vacation rental operators with repetitive guest questions
  • Booking businesses where customers need help choosing
  • Operators who already have a site but need it to do more

Do not lead with

  • Patents
  • AI ecosystem talk
  • Marketplace complexity
  • Huge future roadmap

How to explain the parts

Use plain English. Keep the technical side in the background unless they ask.

Voice / chat layer

The customer-facing interaction. This is what helps them ask questions and get guidance.

Structured data layer

This is why the answers are useful. The agent reads current services, policies, pricing, hours, offers, and availability rules.

Booking bridge

The agent does not have to replace the booking platform. It just routes people into the right option faster.

How to handle objections

Keep the response tight and practical.

“We already have a booking system.”

Good. This sits on top of it. We are not trying to replace your stack. We are making the site more useful before the booking happens.

“We already answer those questions.”

That is the point. Your team should not have to answer the same low-value questions over and over if the site can handle them first.

“What if it is wrong?”

It is built on structured business data and approved knowledge, not random guessing. You start narrow, test it, and expand as it proves itself.

Use this in sales

The clean closing ask

“If we could reduce repetitive questions, guide people into the right booking flow, and make the website more useful without replacing your current stack, would it be worth testing on a pilot?”

Lead with the problem. Prove one win. Expand later.
