How Booking Works

4 steps. Customer books in under 2 minutes.

From QR scan to confirmed booking — no phone tag, no back-and-forth. Real-time availability, instant confirmation.

1

Scan QR or tap link

Customer scans the business QR code or clicks a link. Their booking page opens instantly in their phone browser.

2

Pick a date & service

Real-time availability calendar. Choose the rental, charter, tour, or session they want. Prices shown upfront.

3

Enter details & pay

Name, contact, party size, special requests. Secure payment through Stripe. No redirect, no account required.

4

Instant confirmation

Customer gets a confirmation SMS + email. Business dashboard updates in real time. Zero platform fees taken.
