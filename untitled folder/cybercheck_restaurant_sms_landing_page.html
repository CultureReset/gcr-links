How It Works

Four simple steps. No software babysitting.

CyberCheck is built around low-friction restaurant workflows. Instead of logging into a complicated system, you get a text, tap a link, and update only what changed.

1

CyberCheck sends a prompt

Daily or scheduled text prompts remind you to confirm menu items, specials, hours, music, or booking-related details.

2

You tap the link

The update link opens directly to the exact thing you need to edit, instead of dropping you into a giant dashboard.

3

You change what changed

Update prices, swap specials, mark items out, change hours, or confirm tonight’s event info in seconds.

4

Your live info stays current

If you update it, the live version changes. If you do nothing, the previous approved version stays active.
