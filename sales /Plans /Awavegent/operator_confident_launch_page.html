What we are actually offering

Keep the pitch tight. Sell the layer, not the whole empire.

1. Pre-arrival guest concierge

A site-based concierge layer that answers property and arrival questions before the guest reaches the front desk or support team.

2. Local guidance layer

Restaurants, activities, rainy-day options, family ideas, rentals, and other location-specific recommendations that make the site more useful.

3. Support deflection

Reduce repetitive calls, texts, and low-value support interactions by handling the obvious questions automatically.

4. Pilot-first rollout

Start with a property group, building, or limited scope. Prove guest usefulness and staff relief before expanding.

5. Revenue path later

Once the support layer works, add upgrades, local referrals, experiences, and other pre-arrival monetization paths.

6. Works on top of current stack

This is meant to sit on top of the existing booking and PMS environment, not rip out infrastructure that already works.

How to position it in the room

You do not sell “technology.” You sell relief, prep, and a better website experience.

Open with this

“I’m not trying to replace your booking or PMS stack. I’m focused on what happens before the guest arrives — the part where your team still gets flooded with repetitive questions and your website stops being helpful.”

Then say this

“We’re launching a concierge layer that sits on top of the current site, handles common property and area questions, and turns the website into a more useful pre-arrival experience.”

Close like this

“If this took even a meaningful slice of repetitive guest questions off your team on a pilot group of properties, would that be worth testing?”
